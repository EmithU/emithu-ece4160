Results

Below is the results for the point [-3, -2], along with a video of the car on that point. The car seems to have essentially nailed the point, with it's predicted point falling directly on the ground truth position. Nothing ever works this perfectly 100% of the time, but it was nice to see the car land this.

Below is the results for the point [0,3]. It landed this one perfectly too, which is nice. My car spins pretty well on-axis, which is certainly helping out here. Furthermore, I take the average of multiple ToF readings at each angle, which definitely also helps with how clean the data is coming out.

Below is the results for [5,-3]. As expected, we finally have a point that isn't pefect, but this is still pretty close. The close proximity to the wall makes this point particularly vulnerable to the drift the car has as it rotates. We can even tell that it drifts towards the wall because the predicted point is closer to the wall than the actual point.

Below is the results for [5,3]. This seems to have suffered a similar fate as the above point, and did seem to drift a bit more as it rotated. I suspect that the drift is due to lowering battery levels, and we can see that materialize into a less accurate prediction here. In any case, the prediction is still reasonable.
