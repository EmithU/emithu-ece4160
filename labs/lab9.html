Lab 9

This lab seeks to map out a static room using the ToF sensors on the car.

Control

For this lab, I want to go beyond open-loop control. With that said, the idea of the car collecting ToF measurements while rotating sounds very hard to tune. As such, I plan on implementing orientation control.

For lab 6, I had P-only rotation control as I saw very little steady-state oscillation. I added tape to the wheels; this let the car rotate effectively on carpet and rotate uncontrollably on a smooth surface. The floor of the room of the lab is also a smooth surface, so my lab 6 solution won't work here. I need to back to the drawing board.

I decided to implement full PID control for orientation. The P term will be the main driver for the car's orientation, the I term will help generaliize the car's precision when rotating across surfaces, and the D term will help to prevent overshoot. All 3 are important so the car can rotate as precisely and accurately as possible.
